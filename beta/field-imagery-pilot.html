Boundary overlay and visual inspection only. Google imagery is not sent to the AI or Sentinel analytics service.

Important: Google satellite imagery is not used for NDVI, crop-stress detection, object identification, or model training. Use only the Sentinel-2 output and other appropriately licensed datasets for those analytical tasks. Neither image alone confirms a crop diagnosis.
